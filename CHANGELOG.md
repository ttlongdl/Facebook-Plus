# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Community fork release based on Facebook Plus 1.0.0 by SHAJON-404.

### Added

- Download Reels directly to Photos.
- Download Story photos and videos directly to Photos.
- Settings toggles for Reels and Stories downloading.
- Vietnamese localization.

### Improved

- Reels download button lifecycle and positioning during Reels navigation.
- Hide the Reels download overlay while Facebook Plus settings are presented.
